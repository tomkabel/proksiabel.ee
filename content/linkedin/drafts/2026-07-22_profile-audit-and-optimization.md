## Content strategy

Use four recurring pillars, with one idea per post:

1. Identity failures: phishing-resistant authentication, WebAuthn/FIDO2, QR-login threat models, help-desk abuse.
2. Adversarial architecture: what breaks under a determined attacker and how to redesign it.
3. Public technical work: tools, diagrams, benchmarks, responsible disclosures, and build notes.
4. Secure AI and critical systems: AI supply-chain risk, human-in-the-loop controls, zero trust, and resilient infrastructure.

Recommended cadence: 3 high-quality posts per week for 90 days; write in Estonian or English according to the audience, and add a short English summary when the topic is locally important but internationally relevant.

For enterprise reach, change the tone from “calling out” to “showing the failure and the fix.” Keep evidence, dates, scope, and confidence level explicit. Put external links in the first comment when distribution matters.

Suggested weekly rhythm:

• Tuesday: one technical observation with a diagram.
• Wednesday: one build note or open-tool update.
• Thursday: one buyer-relevant defensive framework or case pattern.
